FLIGHT MANUAL

Fly it with your hands

Origin reads your hands, not a mouse. Here are the eight moves — learn them once and muscle memory does the rest.

1

ARM

Turn on the camera on the next screen — it never leaves your device.

2

SHOW A HAND

Hold one hand up, palm to the lens, inside the frame.

3

PINCH

Touch thumb to index — the model is now yours to move.

One hand · 6
☝

Point · identify

Aim your index finger. The nearest part lights up and names itself.

🤏

Pinch · grab

Thumb to index, then move — the part comes with your hand. Open to let go.

✊

Fist · rotate

Close your hand and move it — the whole model turns with you.

🖐

Open · drop

An open hand drops the part you're holding. Drop it over the 🗑 to remove it.

◧

Flat palm · section

Hold a flat palm to slice the model open and see what's inside.

👍

Thumbs-up · reset

Hold it for a second to snap everything back to the start.

Both hands · 2
🖐🖐

Two palms · zoom

Both hands open. Spread them apart to zoom in, together to zoom out.

✊✊

Two fists · explode

Pull two fists apart to blow the model into parts; twist them to roll it.
